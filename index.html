OUR CAMPUS

A Campus That Cares for Nature

A green campus is an educational environment that promotes environmental protection, sustainable living and responsible use of natural resources.

Our campus encourages students and staff to take part in activities that make the college cleaner, healthier and more eco-friendly.

CLEAN ENERGY

Powering the Campus with Solar Energy

Solar energy is a clean and renewable source of power. Installing solar panels can help educational institutions reduce their dependence on conventional electricity.

Solar power can be used for classrooms, laboratories, lighting and other campus facilities.

Smart Waste Management

Proper waste management keeps the campus clean and reduces environmental pollution.

01

Wet Waste

Food waste, vegetable peels and other biodegradable materials can be collected separately and composted.

02

Dry Waste

Paper, cardboard, plastic and other recyclable materials can be separated for proper recycling.

03

E-Waste

Old electronic devices should be collected separately and sent to authorized recycling facilities.
